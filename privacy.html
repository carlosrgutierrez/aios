AIOS privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

What AIOS accesses

AIOS uses the Gmail read-only permission on the account of its owner, Carlos Gutierrez. It cannot send, change or delete email.

How the data is used

Work-related email threads (clients, partners and known contacts) are copied to a database on the owner's own computer so they can be searched and linked to client records. Personal, promotional and automated email is filtered out and not stored.

Sharing

The owner can search this data through AI assistants he connects himself (such as Claude or ChatGPT); relevant excerpts are sent to them only when he asks a question. Otherwise, Gmail data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. AIOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removal

The owner can revoke access at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Stored copies can be deleted on request.
